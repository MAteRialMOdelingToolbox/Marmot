Gradient-enhanced microplane model M7 (GM7)

Theory

GM7 is a nonlocal, gradient-enhanced extension of the microplane model M7 (Caner and Bažant, 2013) for regularized, mesh-independent softening. It is a MarmotMaterialGeneralGradientEnhancedHypoElastic with six nonlocal fields: the nonlocal macroscopic strain \(\bar{\eps}\), which is the solution of the implicit gradient equation driven by the local strain \(\eps\) with the interaction parameter \(c\).

The elastic predictor on the microplanes is evaluated with the local strain, while the tensile, deviatoric, volumetric and shear boundaries are evaluated with the over-nonlocal strain increment

\[\Delta \hat{\eps} = m \, \Delta \bar{\eps} + \left( 1 - m \right) \Delta \eps,\]

in which \(m\) weights the nonlocal against the local strain. The consistent tangents with respect to the local and the nonlocal strain follow accordingly.

The microplane discretization is taken from the shared Marmot::ContinuumMechanics::Microplane module (37-microplane scheme N37, fixed at compile time). The closed-form transition function for the elastic unloading stiffness and the gradient enhancement of the boundaries are introduced in Basche et al. (2026). The constitutive laws of M7 are implemented once in MicroplaneM7Formulation and shared with MicroplaneM7; GM7 therefore requires the MicroplaneM7 module.

Properties

The fixed parameters of Caner and Bažant (2013) are calibrated in N and mm. Therefore \(E\), \(f_c\) and transitionWidth have to be given in N/mm² (MPa); the M7 parameters \(\nu\), \(k_1, \ldots, k_5\), \(c_3\) and transitionExponent are dimensionless.

The material is registered as GM7 in the general gradient-enhanced hypoelastic material factory (six nonlocal fields), and is used with the six-field GeneralGradientEnhancedDisplacementFiniteElement elements (G6GCPS8, G6GC3D8, G6GC3D20, …).

The state variables are the volumetric stress sig_v0, zeta, the microplane stresses sig_N0, sig_M0, sig_L0, the extremal over-nonlocal normal strains eps_N0pos, eps_N0neg, the macroscopic over-nonlocal strain strain_macro0OverNonLocal and the macroscopic local strain strain_macro0Local, which is returned as the local driving variable of the nonlocal fields. For postprocessing, activeTensileBoundary, activeCompressiveBoundary and activeShearBoundary hold the fractions of the microplanes on the tensile, compressive and shear boundary in the last increment.

Nonlocal gradient-enhanced extension of the microplane model M7 for 3D stress states.

The base formulation follows Caner & Bažant (2013), “Microplane model M7 for

plain concrete. I: Formulation” and “II: Calibration and verification”, Journal of Engineering Mechanics, 139(12). The tensile, deviatoric, volumetric and shear boundaries are evaluated on an “over-nonlocal” strain (see

stress_update) that blends the local strain with a nonlocally-averaged strain via the weighting parameter m, with the nonlocal interaction parameter c, for regularized (mesh-independent) softening. Both the closed-form transition function of the elastic unloading stiffness (see MicroplaneM7Formulation::transitionFunctionClosedForm), improving convergence behavior in implicit simulations, and the nonlocal gradient-enhancement of the boundaries, are introduced in Basche et al. (2026). The constitutive laws of M7 are shared with MicroplaneM7 through MicroplaneM7Formulation.
