Linear Viscoelastic Wiechert model

Theory

The model is an isotropic, linear viscoelastic material in the small-strain (hypoelastic) setting. Its relaxation behaviour is a power law that is represented by a generalized Maxwell chain (Wiechert model) with logarithmically spaced relaxation times. The stress is obtained from the strain history by the hereditary integral

\[\sig(t) = \int_0^t \Psi(t - t')\, \DelNu : \dot{\eps}(t')\, \mathrm{d}t', \qquad \Psi(t) = E_\infty + m\, t^{-n},\]

with the equilibrium Young’s modulus \(E_\infty = E\), the power-law relaxation parameter \(m\), the power-law exponent \(n\), and the unit stiffness tensor

\[\DelNu = \Cel(E=1, \nu),\]

where \(\nu\) is the Poisson’s ratio. The Poisson’s ratio is constant in time.

The decaying part \(m\,t^{-n}\) is replaced by a finite sum of exponentials,

\[\Psi_N(t) = E + E_0 + \sum_{i=1}^{N} E_i\, e^{-t/\tau_i},\]

with \(N\) Maxwell branches. The relaxation times are spaced logarithmically,

\[\tau_i = \tau_{\min}\, s^{\,i-1}, \qquad s = \sqrt{10}, \qquad i = 1, \dots, N,\]

with \(\tau_{\min}\) the smallest relaxation time. The branch moduli \(E_i\) follow from the Post-Widder inversion formula applied to \(m\,t^{-n}\) (order \(k = 2\)), multiplied by the logarithmic spacing \(\ln s\); compare the Linear Viscoelastic Power Law model, which uses the same idea for the creep compliance and a Kelvin chain. The additional stiffness \(E_0\) accounts for the part of the spectrum slower than the slowest resolved branch, so that the tangent stays consistent with the power law beyond \(\tau_N\).

Each branch carries a 6-component stress-like state variable that is updated exponentially over the time increment \(\Delta t\),

\[\boldsymbol{q}_i^{n+1} = \lambda_i\, E_i\, \DelNu : \Delta\eps + \beta_i\, \boldsymbol{q}_i^{n}, \qquad \beta_i = e^{-\Delta t/\tau_i},\]

with \(\lambda_i\) the standard exponential-integrator coefficient of branch \(i\) (see computeLambdaAndBeta). The stress increment and the consistent tangent are

\[\Delta\sig = \mathbb{C}_{\mathrm{eff}} : \Delta\eps - \sum_i (1-\beta_i)\, \boldsymbol{q}_i^{n}, \qquad \mathbb{C}_{\mathrm{eff}} = \Big(E + E_0 + \sum_i \lambda_i E_i\Big)\, \DelNu .\]

Since the Poisson’s ratio is constant, \(\DelNu\) is computed once at construction and only scaled by the effective modulus in every stress update.

The material is registered as LINEARVISCOELASTICWIECHERT in the hypoelastic material factory. Because it is a Marmot::Materials::MaterialHypoElastic, it can also serve as the bulk material of the interface material. The state variables are the 6 * nMaxwell branch stresses.

Isotropic linear viscoelastic material using a generalized Maxwell chain.

A power-law relaxation function Psi(t)=m t^(-n) is approximated by logarithmically spaced Maxwell branches. E is the equilibrium Young’s modulus.

Material properties are ordered as [E, nu, m, n, nMaxwell, minTau, timeToDays, optional density].
