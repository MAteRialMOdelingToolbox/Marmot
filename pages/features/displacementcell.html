Displacement Cell

Theory

The displacement cell is the MPM background cell of the finite-strain displacement formulation. It carries the displacement field (nDim dofs per node) and assembles the residual and the tangent of the Displacement Material Point instances it currently hosts. Cell geometries (Lagrangian and B-spline), the cell interface and the MPM workflow are described in MPM Cells and Material Points; the RKPM counterpart of this formulation is the Displacement Particle (see Meshfree Particles and Meshfree Approximation).

As usual in MPM, the grid dofs \(\Delta q_{Bk}\) are the increments of the current step, and the material points carry the accumulated state. The cell nodes define the intermediate configuration \(\boldsymbol{Y}\) (the configuration at the beginning of the increment). When the material points are assigned, the cell evaluates at the position \(\boldsymbol{Y}_p\) of each material point the shape functions \(N_A\) and their gradients \(\partial N_A/\partial Y_J\), and caches them. Each material point then receives

\[\Delta\boldsymbol{u}_p = N_B\,\Delta\boldsymbol{q}_B, \qquad \Delta F_{iJ} = \delta_{iJ} + \Delta q_{Bi}\,\frac{\partial N_B}{\partial Y_J}.\]

The momentum balance is assembled with the Kirchhoff stress \(\boldsymbol{\tau}\) of the material points, the spatial gradients \(\partial N_A/\partial x_i = \Delta F^{-1}_{Ji}\,\partial N_A/\partial Y_J\) and the undeformed volumes \(V_p^0\) of the material points (the weak form in the undeformed configuration, evaluated at the material points),

\[r_{Aj} = \sum_p \frac{\partial N_A}{\partial x_i}\,\tau_{ij}\,V_p^0 ,\]

with the consistent tangent, consisting of the material part and the geometric stiffness,

\[\frac{\partial r_{Aj}}{\partial \Delta q_{Bk}} = \sum_p \left( \frac{\partial N_A}{\partial x_i}\,\frac{\partial \tau_{ij}}{\partial \Delta F_{kL}}\, \frac{\partial N_B}{\partial Y_L} - \frac{\partial N_A}{\partial x_k}\,\tau_{ij}\,\frac{\partial N_B}{\partial x_i} \right) V_p^0 ,\]

where \(\partial\tau_{ij}/\partial\Delta F_{kL}\) is provided by the material point. The cell does not contain an inertia term in the residual; it provides the consistent mass matrix \(M_{AiBi} = \sum_p N_A\,N_B\,\rho_0\,V_p^0\) and the lumped mass (its row sums) to the host.

class DisplacementCell : public CellBase, public GeometryCell

MPM background cell for finite-strain displacement material points.

The cell carries the displacement field (nDim dofs per node) and consumes DisplacementMaterialPoint instances, which drive a Marmot::Materials::MaterialFiniteStrain. As usual in MPM, the grid dofs \( \Delta q_{Bk} \) are the INCREMENTS of the current step, and the material points carry the accumulated state. The cell nodes define the intermediate configuration \( \boldsymbol{Y} \) (the configuration at the beginning of the increment); the shape functions \( N_A \) and their gradients \( \partial N_A/\partial Y_J \) are evaluated once per material point in assignMaterialPoints(). Each material point receives

\[ \Delta\boldsymbol{u} = N_B\,\Delta\boldsymbol{q}_B, \qquad \Delta F_{iJ} = \delta_{iJ} + \Delta q_{Bi}\,\frac{\partial N_B}{\partial Y_J}. \]
The momentum balance is assembled with the Kirchhoff stress, the spatial gradients \( \partial N_A/\partial x_i = \Delta F^{-1}_{Ji}\,\partial N_A/\partial Y_J \), and the undeformed volumes \( V_p^0 \) of the material points,
\[ r_{Aj} = \sum_p \frac{\partial N_A}{\partial x_i}\,\tau_{ij}\,V_p^0, \]
with the tangent (material part and geometric stiffness)
\[ \frac{\partial r_{Aj}}{\partial \Delta q_{Bk}} = \sum_p \left( \frac{\partial N_A}{\partial x_i}\,\frac{\partial \tau_{ij}}{\partial \Delta F_{kL}}\, \frac{\partial N_B}{\partial Y_L} - \frac{\partial N_A}{\partial x_k}\,\tau_{ij}\,\frac{\partial N_B}{\partial x_i} \right) V_p^0 . \]
It is the displacement-only sibling of GradientEnhancedFiniteStrainCell.

Template Parameters:

  • nDim – Spatial dimension (2: plane strain, 3).

  • nNodes – Number of cell nodes.

  • CellBase – Base class, Marmot::Discretizations::Cells::Cell.

  • GeometryCell – Geometry policy, Lagrangian or B-spline.

void computeDistributedLoad(int type, int surfaceID, int materialPointNumber, const double *load, double *fExt, double *dExt_dQ, double timeNew, double dT) const

Distributed load at a single material point of the cell.

The load vector \( \boldsymbol{f}_0 \) (e.g. \( p\,\boldsymbol{N}\,dA_0 \) in the undeformed configuration) is transformed with Nanson’s formula and the total deformation gradient, \( \boldsymbol{f} = J\,\boldsymbol{F}^{-\mathsf T}\boldsymbol{f}_0 \), and assembled as \( r_{Aj} \mathrel{-}= N_A\,f_j \), with the corresponding load stiffness.

Parameters:

  • type – [in] The distributed load type (Pressure).

  • surfaceID – [in] Surface ID (not used).

  • materialPointNumber – [in] Label of the material point the load acts on; other material points are skipped.

  • load – [in] The load vector \( \boldsymbol{f}_0 \) (nDim values).

  • fExt – [inout] Load vector, the contribution is added.

  • dExt_dQ – [inout] Tangent, the contribution is added.

  • timeNew – [in] Time at the end of the increment.

  • dT – [in] Time increment.

Throws:

std::invalid_argument – for an unsupported type.
