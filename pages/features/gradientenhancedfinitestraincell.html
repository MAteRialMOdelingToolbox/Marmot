Gradient-Enhanced Finite-Strain Cell

Theory

The gradient-enhanced finite-strain cell is the MPM background cell of the implicit-gradient finite-strain formulation. It carries the displacement field \(\boldsymbol u\) (nDim dofs per node) and a scalar nonlocal field \(\bar N\) (1 dof per node) and assembles the contributions of the Gradient-Enhanced Finite-Strain Material Point instances it currently hosts, which in turn drive a material of the interface Marmot::Materials::MaterialGradientEnhancedFiniteStrain (e.g. Gradient-enhanced compressible Neo-Hooke damage, Gradient-enhanced finite-strain Drucker-Prager). It is the MPM counterpart of Gradient-Enhanced Finite-Strain Displacement Element; the cell geometries and the MPM workflow are described in MPM Cells and Material Points.

Kinematics

As usual in MPM, the grid dofs are the increments \(\Delta\boldsymbol q\) of the current step, while the material points carry the accumulated state. When the material points are assigned, the cell evaluates its shape functions \(N_A\) and their gradients \(\nabla_Y N_A\) at the position of each material point in the intermediate reference configuration \(\boldsymbol Y\) (the last accepted configuration). The increment is interpolated to each point (interpolateFieldsToMaterialPoints),

\[\Delta\boldsymbol u_p = N_A\,\Delta\boldsymbol q^U_A, \qquad \frac{\partial\Delta u_i}{\partial Y_j} = \Delta q^U_{Ai}\,\frac{\partial N_A}{\partial Y_j}, \qquad \Delta\bar N_p = N_A\,\Delta q^N_A,\]

and the spatial and the undeformed gradients follow with the increment \(\Delta\boldsymbol F\) and the accepted deformation gradient \(\boldsymbol F_n\) of the material point,

\[\frac{\partial N_A}{\partial x_i} = \frac{\partial N_A}{\partial Y_j}\,\Delta F^{-1}_{ji}, \qquad \frac{\partial N_A}{\partial X_i} = \frac{\partial N_A}{\partial Y_j}\,F_{n,ji} .\]

Weak forms

Summing over the material points \(p\) with their undeformed volumes \(V^0_p\), the momentum balance is assembled with the Kirchhoff stress,

\[r_{U,Aj} = \sum_p \frac{\partial N_A}{\partial x_i}\,\tau_{ij}\,V^0_p ,\]

and the nonlocal balance \(\bar N - c\,\nabla_X^2\bar N = L\) in the undeformed configuration, in increment form,

\[r_{N,A} = \sum_p \Bigl( N_A\,\Delta\bar N_p + c\,\nabla_X N_A\cdot\nabla_X\Delta\bar N_p - N_A\,\Delta L_p \Bigr) V^0_p ,\]

with \(\nabla_X\Delta\bar N_p = \nabla_X N_B\,\Delta q^N_B\), the change \(\Delta L_p\) of the local driving force reported by the material point and \(c = R^2\) from the material. The undeformed gradient \(\nabla_X\) does not depend on the current increment. The residual is the internal force vector; body forces and pressures are added with a negative sign.

Tangent

With the tangents of the material point with respect to \(\Delta\boldsymbol F\) and \(\bar N\), the blocks of the consistent tangent are

\[\begin{split}K^{UU}_{jAkB} &= \sum_p \Bigl( \frac{\partial N_A}{\partial x_i}\,\frac{\partial\tau_{ij}}{\partial\Delta F_{kL}}\, \frac{\partial N_B}{\partial Y_L} - \frac{\partial N_A}{\partial x_k}\,\tau_{ij}\, \frac{\partial N_B}{\partial x_i} \Bigr) V^0_p ,\\ K^{UN}_{jAB} &= \sum_p \frac{\partial N_A}{\partial x_i}\,\frac{\partial\tau_{ij}}{\partial\bar N}\,N_B\,V^0_p ,\\ K^{NU}_{AkB} &= -\sum_p N_A\,\frac{\partial L}{\partial\Delta F_{kL}}\,\frac{\partial N_B}{\partial Y_L}\,V^0_p ,\\ K^{NN}_{AB} &= \sum_p \Bigl( N_A N_B\,\bigl(1 - \tfrac{\partial L}{\partial\bar N}\bigr) + c\,\nabla_X N_A\cdot\nabla_X N_B \Bigr) V^0_p ,\end{split}\]

where the second term of \(K^{UU}\) is the geometric stiffness from the dependence of \(\nabla_x N_A\) on \(\Delta\boldsymbol F\). The interaction \(c\) is treated as constant.

Loads and inertia

  • BODYFORCE: \(f_{Ai} \mathrel{-}= \sum_p N_A\,b_i\,V^0_p\) with the body force \(\boldsymbol b\) per unit undeformed volume, no load stiffness.

  • PRESSURE: a follower load acting on a single material point (selected by its label). The undeformed load vector \(\boldsymbol f_0 = p\,\boldsymbol N\,dA_0\) is pushed forward by Nanson’s formula with the total deformation gradient \(\boldsymbol F = \Delta\boldsymbol F\,\boldsymbol F_n\), \(\boldsymbol f = J\,\boldsymbol F^{-\mathsf T}\boldsymbol f_0\), and applied as \(f_{Ai} \mathrel{-}= N_A f_i\), with the consistent load stiffness.

  • Inertia: the consistent mass \(M_{AiBi} = \sum_p N_A N_B\,\rho_0 V^0_p\) of the displacement field and its row-sum lumped version. The nonlocal field carries no inertia.

The dofs are ordered field by field inside the cell (all displacements, then all nonlocal dofs); getDofIndicesPermutationPattern maps them to the node-by-node layout of the host.

Registered types

The cells are registered in the Marmot::Registry::CellFactory (Lagrangian, createCell) and as B-spline cells (createBSplineCell). The nodal fields are displacement and nonlocal damage.

MPM background cell for gradient-enhanced (implicit-gradient) finite-strain materials.

The cell carries the displacement field \( \boldsymbol{u} \) (nDim dofs per node) and one scalar nonlocal field \( \bar{N} \) (1 dof per node, field name nonlocal damage), and consumes Marmot::Discretizations::MaterialPoints::GradientEnhancedFiniteStrainMaterialPoint instances, which in turn drive a Marmot::Materials::MaterialGradientEnhancedFiniteStrain. It is the MPM counterpart of the finite element GradientEnhancedFiniteStrainDisplacementElement.

As usual in MPM, the grid dofs are the INCREMENTS of the current step, \( \Delta\boldsymbol{q} \); the material points carry the accumulated state. The cell shape functions \( N_A \) and their gradients \( \nabla_Y N_A \) are evaluated once, at the position of each material point in the intermediate reference configuration \( \boldsymbol{Y} \) (the last accepted configuration), when the material points are assigned. The spatial and the reference gradients follow from

\[ \frac{\partial N_A}{\partial x_i} = \frac{\partial N_A}{\partial Y_j}\,\Delta F^{-1}_{ji}, \qquad \frac{\partial N_A}{\partial X_i} = \frac{\partial N_A}{\partial Y_j}\,F_{n,ji} . \]
The momentum balance is integrated with the Kirchhoff stress over the undeformed volume \( V_p^0 \) of each material point \( p \),
\[ r_{U,Aj} = \sum_p \frac{\partial N_A}{\partial x_i}\,\tau_{ij}\,V_p^0 , \]
and the nonlocal balance \( \bar{N} - c\,\nabla_X^2\bar{N} = L \) is assembled in increment form in the undeformed configuration,
\[ r_{N,A} = \sum_p \Bigl( N_A\,\Delta\bar{N}_p + c\,\nabla_X N_A \cdot \nabla_X \Delta\bar{N}_p - N_A\,\Delta L_p \Bigr) V_p^0 , \]
with \( \Delta\bar{N}_p = N_B\,\Delta\bar{N}_B \) interpolated from the grid increments, \( \Delta L_p \) the change of the local driving force reported by the material point, and \( c = R^2 \) from the material. The reference gradient \( \nabla_X \) does not depend on the current increment.

The increment form is used because the grid is reset every increment, so its nodal values carry only the increment of the step; the total form of the element of the finite element method, \( N_A(\bar{N} - L) + c\,\nabla_X N_A\cdot\nabla_X\bar{N} \), would need \( \nabla_X\bar{N} \) as an accumulated state of the material point. It thus assumes that the equation of the previous increment holds with the current grid and point positions; storing \( \nabla_X\bar{N} \) instead was tested and changed the nonlocal field by less than the step dependence of the kinematics, without bringing it closer to the result of a single increment.

The consistent tangent consists of

\[ K^{UU}_{jAkB} = \sum_p \Bigl( \frac{\partial N_A}{\partial x_i}\, \frac{\partial\tau_{ij}}{\partial\Delta F_{kL}}\,\frac{\partial N_B}{\partial Y_L} - \frac{\partial N_A}{\partial x_k}\,\tau_{ij}\,\frac{\partial N_B}{\partial x_i} \Bigr) V_p^0 ,\qquad K^{UN}_{jAB} = \sum_p \frac{\partial N_A}{\partial x_i}\,\frac{\partial\tau_{ij}}{\partial\bar{N}}\,N_B\,V_p^0 , \]
\[ K^{NU}_{AkB} = -\sum_p N_A\,\frac{\partial L}{\partial\Delta F_{kL}}\,\frac{\partial N_B}{\partial Y_L}\,V_p^0 , \qquad K^{NN}_{AB} = \sum_p \Bigl( N_A N_B \bigl( 1 - \frac{\partial L}{\partial\bar{N}} \bigr) + c\,\nabla_X N_A\cdot\nabla_X N_B \Bigr) V_p^0 . \]
The interaction \( c \) is treated as constant in the tangent.

Dofs are ordered field by field internally (all displacements, then all nonlocal dofs); see getDofIndicesPermutationPattern() for the mapping to the node-by-node layout of the host.
